# Midnight Core Constitution

1. No normal protected merge without a valid authorization source.
2. No production change without traceable ownership.
3. AI may execute engineering work but may not approve human responsibility gates.
4. Machine-derived governance state must not be manually edited.
5. Blocking ambiguity prevents execution authorization.
6. ReleaseRevision becomes immutable when Candidate is created.
7. Evidence must bind the exact governed subject.
8. G3 verifier and required G4 approver responsibilities must remain separated.
9. Accepted bundle must equal released bundle.
10. Waiver must never convert a technical fail into pass.
11. Emergency bypass must remain observable.
12. Historical governance facts must never be rewritten.
13. Tool-native instruction files are generated projections; `.midnight/` is canonical.
14. A language model's statement that a deterministic rule passed is not evidence that it passed.
